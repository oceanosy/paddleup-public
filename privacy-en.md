Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Version: 20261029

Clarified November 13, 2026 (KST): this update clarifies existing processing of crew relationships, chat and reports, and the transmission of search filters to the server and their possible inclusion in operational and diagnostic logs. ## Scope and notice of changes

This policy applies to PaddleUP for Android and iOS and its connected watch features. From October 29, 2026, it includes privacy practices for member features. The guest scoreboard remains available without signing in. Member features depend on the app version and the readiness of connected services. Publishing this policy does not mean that every feature has launched.

## Guest scoreboard, voice and watches

Team names, scores, serving state, match settings and on-device records entered by guests are stored on the device. When a member chooses to save a record, that record is stored on the member server. Match state and control results are sent to compatible watches. Spoken scores use the app’s English audio playback and do not record the microphone. Local data can be removed using in-app deletion features or by clearing the app’s data in device settings.

## Information processed for signup and sign-in

Account identifiers, verified email addresses and nicknames provided by Apple, Google or Kakao, together with the nickname you set, are used for signup, authentication and account management. If you use Apple’s private email feature, the relay address is processed. Profile photos are optional and are processed only when you consent to provide them. PaddleUP does not store your social-account passwords on its servers or require a separate signup password.

## Member features and visibility

We use the bio, region, match records, crews, posts, comments, chat, reports and blocking information you enter to provide those features and operate the service safely. Email addresses and sign-in account identifiers are not displayed in public profiles. Nicknames and selected photos may be displayed to other members. Content visibility depends on the feature screen and crew membership, among other factors. Match records are managed under their owner’s account. Signup does not request access to Kakao friend lists, KakaoTalk messages or calendars. We store in-app activity and member relationships, such as likes, follows, crew membership and votes, to provide those social features. Crew chat stores message content and author and crew information to deliver messages to members of that crew. These features do not read your device’s address book. For reports, we store the reason, reporting account identifier, identifier of the reported content and handling status to respond to reports and operate the service safely.

## Locations you enter for crews and meetups

We store the country or territory, province or city, city or playing area, and venue or court name that you select or enter for crews and VS meetups to display them and support searches. Other members who are permitted to view the relevant crew or meetup can see and search this information. This feature does not infer your location from device GPS or your IP address and does not require your home address. The selected country does not determine the app display language. The country, region and filters you select or enter when searching for crews and match meetups are sent to our server to provide search results. Search requests may also appear in operational logs used to operate the service and diagnose errors.

## Request account deletion

You can request account deletion in the app by going to Member home → Profile → Account management → Withdraw, and confirming. Even if you cannot access the app, you can request deletion of your PaddleUP account by emailing sean.oh.sy@gmail.com. Tell us the sign-in email address and sign-in provider (Apple, Google or Kakao) for the account you want deleted. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We will guide you through deletion after verifying your identity. The sections below on “Retention and account withdrawal”, “Incomplete Kakao signup and deletion verification”, and “Service providers and processing locations” describe what is deleted, what may remain, and the applicable retention and cleanup arrangements.

## Retention and account withdrawal

Member information and saved records are retained while the account is maintained and the features are provided. On account withdrawal, we delete the personal information and content you own in the service database. Comments attached to your posts may also be deleted depending on data relationships. Content independently created by other members is not deleted in bulk. Crew owners may need to complete crew-management steps first. If you cannot withdraw directly, you can request deletion using the contact information below. Inquiry information is used to respond and deleted after handling. Where a separate statutory retention obligation applies, the information is stored separately according to the applicable legal basis and period.

## Incomplete Kakao signup and deletion verification

Information received before you confirm Kakao signup is temporarily processed for up to 10 minutes and deleted by cleanup jobs after expiry. When a cancellation request is received, the temporary personal information is erased. On withdrawal, we delete email, nickname, photo URL, Kakao identifiers and consent records. A randomly assigned account identifier and job status used to recheck deletion and prevent delayed jobs are subject to a 24-hour verification window after withdrawal. They are removed in the next cleanup cycle after authentication-account deletion and reference removal are confirmed. Failed deletions are cleaned up after completion is verified. This is not a guarantee that all backups and physical copies disappear at exactly 24 hours.

## Service providers and processing locations

We use Supabase’s Seoul region (ap-northeast-2) for the member database, authentication and storage. Supabase Pte. Ltd. processes data to provide the service. Related providers, including US affiliates, may process data when needed for support and operations. Edge functions, operational logs, service metadata and similar data may be processed outside the selected region. Supabase states that operational logs are collected and stored in EU data centers. We therefore do not guarantee that all processing takes place only in Korea. Authentication, security and operational processing by sign-in and advertising providers is governed by their respective policies. Provider operational logs and recovery copies follow those providers’ deletion schedules. Withdrawing from the app does not mean that those copies are immediately physically deleted.

## Secure processing

We use encryption in transit, account-level access controls and operating-system secure storage. Authentication tokens on the Kakao relay server are managed in encrypted form. IP addresses, device and browser information, and service logs may be processed for authentication, security and incident response. Our relay diagnostic logs are configured to record processing stages rather than raw tokens, email addresses, nicknames and similar information.

## Google advertising

Google AdMob banners appear at the top of screens other than the scoreboard. Interstitial ads appear at the end of a match when ready. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Test environments such as TestFlight may show test ads that do not generate revenue. In regions where consent is required, we provide an advertising consent screen. You can use the advertising privacy settings in the app settings and your device’s advertising and tracking settings. Ads may not appear depending on whether ads are requested and on ad availability.

## Eligibility, rights and contact

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older. Members can request changes to their information or account deletion through account management in the app. For access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or withdrawal of consent, contact sean.oh.sy@gmail.com. We will explain the necessary steps after verifying your identity. If you do not consent to providing member information, you cannot use member features, but you can use the guest scoreboard. The operator cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.
